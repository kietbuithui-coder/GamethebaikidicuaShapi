# THE COLOR THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST

**A Mobile-First Psychological Horror Game**

## Overview

A browser-based psychological horror game set in the city of Vesper. You wake in Apartment 407 with no memory. The city has a sickly green light, the sun casts no shadows, and no one can tell you what the color "red" is.

You have a camera. It shows you things your eyes cannot see.

## How to Play

Open `game/index.html` in any modern web browser. Works on mobile and desktop.

### Controls
- **Tap navigation buttons** at the bottom to move between locations
- **Tap hotspot buttons** to examine objects and interact with the environment
- **Tap NPC figures** to start conversations
- **📷 button** — Open the camera (shows anomalies invisible to the naked eye)
- **🖼 button** — Open your photo album
- **📓 button** — Open investigation notes
- **⏎ button** — Save/Load game

### Core Mechanics

1. **The Camera** — View the world through the lens to see hidden anomalies. Take photographs to record evidence.
2. **Photographs** — Photos may change when you view them multiple times or after sleeping. Some contain the unnamed color.
3. **Dialogue** — Choose your words carefully. Trust is earned and lost. Every choice has consequences.
4. **Day/Night Cycle** — Events unfold over 7 days. Some events only happen at night.
5. **Save System** — 3 save slots. Auto-saves when you close the tab.

### Endings

There are 10 unique endings based on your choices:
- The Perfect Photograph
- Everyone Remembers You
- The Last Red Image
- Nothing Was Wrong
- The Witness
- The Eraser
- The Believer
- The Impostor
- The Colorless
- The Loop

Each playthrough can reach different endings based on trust levels, evidence collected, photos taken, and choices made.

## Design Philosophy

- No cheap jumpscares
- Horror through atmosphere and implication
- The camera reveals truth but also changes reality
- NPCs have their own motivations and secrets
- Photos are evidence that can be trusted... mostly
- Every choice matters, even seemingly small ones
- The color red is the game's central mystery

## Technical

- Pure HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript
- Canvas 2D rendering
- Web Audio API for atmospheric sound
- LocalStorage for save data
- Mobile-first responsive design
- No external dependencies
- ~125KB total

## Horror Phenomena

1. **The Borrowed Face** — Entities that copy NPC appearances imperfectly
2. **The Empty Room** — A room that moves between locations
3. **The Second Shadow** — Shadows that lag behind their owners
4. **The Unphotographable** — Something that erases people from photos
5. **The Color Leak** — Red appearing in photographs, changing reality
6. **The Loop** — Events repeating with small variations
7. **The Approaching Child** — Gets closer each time photographed
8. **The Memory Photograph** — Photos change after sleeping
